# Content Strategy & Search Intent Keyword Mapping

## 1. Target Audience & Search Intent Profiles

SpiderChat serves two primary search intents:

1. **Transactional / Utilitarian Intent:** Users looking for instant, anonymous, zero-friction temporary web chat rooms without account creation or phone numbers.
2. **Informational & Aesthetic Intent:** Enthusiasts looking for unique comic-themed web apps, Spider-Verse aesthetic UI, and privacy-first ephemeral messaging.

## 3. Helpful Content Standards & Anti-AI-Spam Policy

* **Authentic Product Details:** Content directly references the real WebSocket implementation, room lifecycle timers, and privacy guarantees.
* **Elimination of Fake Claims:** No fabricated user reviews or false encryption claims.
* **Original FAQ Content:** Added real, helpful answers addressing how room memory eviction functions and why guest rooms require no login.
